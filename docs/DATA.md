# Data: what is real, what is simulated, and how

Concord runs on one simulated clinical program as five source systems would export it. Wherever public
data exists it is used unchanged; the rest is generated by `server/scripts/simulate_feeds.py` from that real data,
with a fixed seed, so the feeds in this repository can be reproduced byte for byte.

Everything is evaluated as of the **snapshot date, 2014-01-31**. The real trial data runs from 2012 to
2015; cutting it at the snapshot makes the trial "live", with 55 subjects still on study, so that
questions about open cases, drug supply and overdue payments mean something.

## Public sources

Retrieved by `server/scripts/fetch_public_data.py` into `server/data/public/`. URLs, retrieval times and SHA-256 hashes
are in `server/data/public/SOURCES.json`.

| Source | What we took | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| [CDISC SDTM/ADaM pilot project](https://github.com/cdisc-org/sdtm-adam-pilot-project) (study CDISCPILOT01) | SDTM domains DM, AE, SV, DS, converted from SAS transport files to CSV with no other change | The EDC feed |
| [ClinicalTrials.gov API v2](https://clinicaltrials.gov/data-api/about-api), study NCT07011732 | The study record and its United States locations (city, state, postal code, coordinates) | The CTMS study record and site geography |
| [openFDA drug adverse event API](https://open.fda.gov/apis/drug/event/) | Three real reports, as a template for the record layout only | The shape of the safety feed |

The CDISC pilot study has no registry entry. NCT07011732 is a real study of a xanomeline product in
Alzheimer's disease and is borrowed only for registry fields and site geography; the pairing with the
pilot data is ours and is stated in the CTMS study record itself.

## The five feeds

Each folder under `server/feeds/` has a `_manifest.json` giving the system's owner and steward, when it was
extracted, its freshness SLA, its id conventions, and the sensitivity tags the owner places on its files
and columns.

| Feed | Files | Rows | Real or simulated |
|---|---|---|---|
| `edc` | `dm.csv`, `ae.csv`, `sv.csv`, `ds.csv` | 270 subjects, 997 adverse events, 2,795 visits, 412 dispositions | **Real** CDISC pilot rows, filtered to records dated on or before the snapshot. End dates after the snapshot are blanked |
| `ctms` | `study.json`, `sites.csv`, `monitoring_visits.csv` | 17 sites, 160 monitoring visits | Geography **real**. Site names, investigators, monitors and visits simulated |
| `irt` | `randomization.csv`, `kits.csv` | 229 randomizations, 1,535 kits | Simulated from the real arms and visit dates: one kit per dispensing visit, plus shelf stock per site |
| `safety` | `cases.json` | 37 cases | Simulated from the real serious and severe adverse events, in the openFDA layout with added `study` and `case` blocks |
| `finance` | `vendors.csv`, `site_payments.csv` | 18 vendors, 96 payment documents | Simulated: one invoice per site per quarter, priced off the real visit counts |

### How the ids disagree

| | Study | Site | Subject | Dates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| EDC | `CDISCPILOT01` | `701` | `01-701-1015` | `2014-01-02` |
| CTMS | `CDISC-PILOT-01` | `SITE-0701` | none | `01/02/2014` |
| IRT | `PILOT01` | `US701` | `7011015` | `02JAN2014` |
| Safety | `CDISCPILOT-01` | free-text name, abbreviated and sometimes misspelled | `1015` | `20140102` |
| Finance | `PRJ-CDP01` | vendor id and legal name | none | `02.01.2014`, amounts as `12,450.00` |

Two sites share a city and differ by one word in their names. That pair is the hard case for name matching
and is deliberately left for a data steward to confirm.

### The story in the data

A few sites are given real operational trouble so that cross-system questions have answers:
open safety cases at six sites, low drug supply at five, overdue payments at six, no recent monitoring at
three. Two sites have all of the first three. The lists are in `server/feeds/ANSWER_KEY.json` under `story_sites`.

## Planted data-quality problems

Twenty-five problems are planted and recorded in `server/feeds/ANSWER_KEY.json`. The pipeline never reads that
file. Only the Quality page and the tests do, to score the rules against it.

| Kind | Planted |
|---|---|
| Duplicates | A payment document listed twice (two of them); a safety case sent twice under two report ids; a site keyed twice in CTMS with a re-typed name |
| Orphans | An IRT randomization for a subject not in EDC; a safety case for a subject not in EDC; a monitoring visit for a site not in the master; a site payment to a courier |
| Stale feed | The finance extract is 24 days old against a 7-day SLA |
| Impossible dates | Two randomizations dated before screening; two kits dispensed after expiry; a payment paid before it was invoiced; a monitoring visit in 2041; a case onset in 2099 |
| Cross-system conflicts | Three reportable events in EDC with no safety case; two cases whose onset date differs from EDC; one case whose patient sex differs from EDC |
| Invalid or missing | A negative payment; a payment with no due date; a safety site name that matches nothing |

The rules also report findings nobody planted. Most are adverse events in the real pilot data whose onset
was collected as a partial date such as `1977` or `2013-06`. The rest are the same planted problems seen
from a second system, for example the courier also appearing as an unmatched vendor.

## Definitions used throughout

- **Reportable event**: an adverse event that is serious or severe, with onset on or after screening. In
  this simulation the sponsor's safety plan requires a case in the safety database for each one.
- **Active subject**: randomized and dosed, with no disposition event as of the snapshot.
- **Low supply**: the site has active subjects and fewer unexpired kits on the shelf than two per active
  subject, enough for two dispensing visits each.
- **Overdue payment**: an open document whose due date is before the snapshot.

## Terms

The CDISC pilot data is published by CDISC for public use. ClinicalTrials.gov and openFDA data are US
government works in the public domain; openFDA asks that its data not be presented as medical advice.
The safety cases in `server/feeds/safety/` are simulated and are not real safety reports.
